## Step 10: Verify Your Business

Google will send a **postcard** with a verification code to your address.

1. Wait 5-14 days for the postcard
2. When it arrives, go to business.google.com
3. Click **"Verify now"**
4. Enter the 5-digit code from the postcard
5. Your business is now verified!

**Note:** You can also request phone verification if available.
