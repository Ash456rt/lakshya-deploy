Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply?

Click "Apply Now" on any course page. Our admissions team will schedule a brief call to understand your goals and recommend the best program.

What are the prerequisites?

Most programs require basic computer literacy. Data Analysis and Web Development accept beginners. Cybersecurity requires some technical background.

Is there financial aid?

Yes! We offer Income Share Agreements, EMI options, and merit-based scholarships. Speak with an advisor for personalized options.

Can I switch programs?

Absolutely. If you discover a different track fits better within the first 2 weeks, we'll help you transition at no extra cost.
