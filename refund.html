Refund Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Eligibility for Refund

Refund eligibility depends on the timing of your cancellation request relative to your enrollment date and course commencement. We are committed to fair refund practices as outlined below.

2. Refund Schedule

Before Course Commencement

Full refund minus ₹2,000 administrative fee if cancellation is made at least 7 days before the course start date.

Within First 7 Days

75% refund of course fees if cancellation is requested within 7 days of course commencement and less than 20% of the course has been completed.

Within First 30 Days

50% refund of course fees if cancellation is requested within 30 days of course commencement and less than 40% of the course has been completed.

After 30 Days

No refund is available for cancellations made after 30 days of course commencement. You may transfer your enrollment to another batch or program (subject to availability and fee difference).

3. Non-Refundable Items

The following are non-refundable: registration fees, any discount or scholarship amounts availed, course materials already downloaded or accessed, and third-party software or tool licenses purchased on your behalf.

4. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, email refunds@lakshya.academy with: your full name, enrollment ID, reason for cancellation, and preferred refund method. We will process your request within 5-7 business days. Refunds are credited to the original payment method.

5. EMI and Loan Payments

If you paid via EMI or education loan, the refund amount will be adjusted against the outstanding loan balance. Any excess will be credited to your account. Contact our finance team for EMI-specific refund queries.

6. Batch Transfer

Instead of a refund, you may request a batch transfer to a future date at no additional cost (subject to availability). One free batch transfer is allowed per enrollment. Subsequent transfers may incur a processing fee.

7. Scholarship and Discount Adjustments

Refund amounts are calculated based on the actual fees paid after any scholarship or discount. If your scholarship has expired, the refund will be based on current course pricing.
