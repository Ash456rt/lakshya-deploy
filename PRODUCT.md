# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Primary:** Business owners and decision-makers in Bengaluru, India (and globally) who need multiple services — web/app development, consultancy, import/export, logistics, travel, customer support, or training — and want one accountable partner instead of juggling seven vendors.
- **Secondary:** Potential employees and training candidates interested in Laksya Academy programs.
- **Tertiary:** Existing clients accessing the Client Portal for project updates.

## Product Purpose

Laksya Groups is a multi-service conglomerate that provides end-to-end business solutions. The website serves as the primary marketing and lead generation channel, showcasing all seven service divisions and converting visitors into consultations. It also hosts the Client Portal for ongoing project collaboration.

## Positioning

"One accountable partner instead of seven vendors." Laksya Groups uniquely bundles development, consultancy, import/export, customer support, logistics, travel, and training under one roof — each service grew from a real client need, not a menu expansion. Based in Bengaluru with operations across 50+ countries.

## Operating Context

- Visitors land from Google search, social media (X, LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube), or referrals
- Primary conversion path: browse services → read case studies/blog → submit contact form or schedule a call
- Client Portal: existing clients log in to track project progress
- Admin dashboard: internal team manages content, contacts, and client data
- Academy and Travels are separate sub-sites with their own HTML pages

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Seven service divisions:** App & Web Development, Strategic Consultancy, Import & Export, Customer Support, Transport & Logistics, Tours & Travel, Lakshya Academy
- **Tech stack:** Next.js 16, React 19, Tailwind CSS 4, Framer Motion, Supabase (auth + database)
- **Contact form:** submits to Supabase database via server action with honeypot spam protection
- **Blog:** CMS-driven with categories, authors, and read times
- **Case Studies:** dedicated section for showcasing work
- **Client Portal:** authenticated area for project updates
- **Admin Panel:** internal management interface
- **SEO:** comprehensive structured data (Organization, WebSite, LocalBusiness), Open Graph, Twitter cards
- **Analytics:** Google Analytics (G-SGR6KY1CMC)
- **No e-commerce:** the site is marketing + lead gen, not transactional

## Product Principles

1. **One partner, seven services** — every service exists because a client needed it, not for menu breadth
2. **Results over rhetoric** — testimonials cite specific outcomes (6-week rebuild, 3hr→10min, 50% coordination cut)
3. **Bengaluru roots, global reach** — local presence with 50+ country delivery
4. **Technical credibility** — the site itself demonstrates development capability (Next.js, animations, modern stack)
5. **Accessible entry** — free consultation, no credit card, 24/7 support messaging
