Frequently asked questions

What does an import-export service provider actually do?+

We handle the operational side of international trade: finding and vetting suppliers, negotiating with freight forwarders, preparing customs documentation, clearing shipments, and delivering to or from your warehouse. You get one accountable partner instead of coordinating agents, brokers and transporters yourself.

Which documents are required for importing into India?+

Typical shipments need a commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading or airway bill, and the correct HS classification for customs duty. Depending on the product, additional certificates apply. We prepare and verify the full set so your goods clear customs without surprises.

Can you source products from India for our business?+

Yes. We identify and audit suppliers, negotiate pricing and terms, arrange quality checks before dispatch, and manage the export side through delivery. Our teams are based in Bengaluru with supplier networks across India and Southeast Asia.

How do you keep shipping costs down?+

Consolidated freight, correct HS classification, planning around peak-season rate surges, and choosing sea vs air deliberately per shipment. Route optimization built into our logistics practice routinely saves clients 10-25% on freight compared to unmanaged shipping.
