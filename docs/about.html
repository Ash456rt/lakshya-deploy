Founder

Sakthivel Saravanan

Founder, Lakshya Groups

Sakthivel built Lakshya Groups from a desks in Bengaluru into a multi-service company with seven divisions and partners across 63 countries. His approach is simple: solve the client's real problem, not just the one they asked about; keep numbers honest; and treat every delivery like it will be used by strangers on a weak phone connection.

Before Lakshya Groups, he spent years building web and mobile products end to end — from the first wireframe to the first paying customer — which is why the company still ships products, not just pages.
