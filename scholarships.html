Scholarships & Financial Aid

We believe financial constraints should not block talent. Explore our scholarship programs designed to make quality tech education accessible to everyone.

Available Scholarships

Merit Excellence Scholarship

Up to 50% tuition waiver

For students with exceptional academic records or outstanding performance in our entrance assessment.

  • Minimum 85% in previous academic qualification
  • Strong performance in Lakshya entrance test
  • Outstanding technical aptitude

Women in Tech Scholarship

Up to 40% tuition waiver

Encouraging women to pursue careers in technology. Dedicated support and mentorship included.

  • Open to all women applicants
  • Demonstrated interest in technology
  • Statement of purpose required

Need-Based Financial Aid

Up to 60% tuition waiver

For students from economically disadvantaged backgrounds who demonstrate strong potential.

  • Family income below threshold
  • Valid documentation required
  • Interview with admissions team

Early Bird Discount

15% early enrollment discount

Enroll early in the upcoming batch and save on your course fees.

  • Enroll at least 30 days before batch start
  • Applicable to all programs
  • Cannot be combined with other scholarships

Referral Scholarship

10% discount for both

Refer a friend and both of you receive a discount on your course fees.

  • Referred student must enroll and pay full fee
  • Both students must be in active programs
  • Unlimited referrals

Alumni Upgrade Scholarship

25% tuition waiver

Special pricing for Lakshya Academy alumni who want to upskill with a new program.

  • Must be a Lakshya Academy graduate
  • For any new program enrollment
  • Lifetime validity

How to Apply

Simple 4-step process to apply for any scholarship.

01

Apply Online

02

Assessment

03

04

Decision
