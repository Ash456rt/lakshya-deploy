Accessibility Statement

Last updated: July 2026

Our Commitment

Lakshya Academy is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We are continually improving the user experience for everyone and applying the relevant accessibility standards.

Conformance Status

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible for people with disabilities and more user-friendly for everyone.

Accessibility Features

✓Keyboard navigation support for all interactive elements
✓Screen reader compatible content and ARIA labels
✓Sufficient color contrast ratios (minimum 4.5:1)
✓Responsive design for all screen sizes and devices
✓Alternative text for all meaningful images
✓Captions and transcripts for video content
✓Reduced motion option for users with vestibular disorders
✓Focus indicators for keyboard navigation

Course Accessibility

Our courses are designed with accessibility in mind. All video content includes captions. Written materials are available in accessible formats. We provide alternative assessment methods for students who need them. If you require specific accommodations, please contact us before enrollment.

Assistive Technology Compatibility

We test our website with the following assistive technologies: NVDA and JAWS screen readers, Dragon NaturallySpeaking voice recognition, keyboard-only navigation, and browser zoom up to 200%.

Known Limitations

We are aware of some accessibility limitations: some older PDF documents may not be fully accessible, certain third-party embedded content may not meet our standards, and some complex interactive visualizations may be difficult to navigate with screen readers. We are actively working to address these issues.

Feedback and Contact

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of our website and courses. If you encounter accessibility barriers or have suggestions for improvement, please contact us:

Email: accessibility@lakshya.academy
Phone: +91 80 1234 5678
We aim to respond to accessibility feedback within 2 business days.

Enforcement Procedure

If you are not satisfied with our response to your accessibility concern, you may escalate the matter by writing to our Accessibility Coordinator at accessibility@lakshya.academy. We take all accessibility complaints seriously and will work to resolve them promptly.
